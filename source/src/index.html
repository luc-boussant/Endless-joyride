How to play

Build rides on the lots and level them up. More fun brings more guests, and guests pay for every ride.
Building takes real time: a few minutes for the first rides, hours and then days for the big ones. Each builder crew works on one thing at a time (new rides, seats, speed, land), and construction carries on while you are away.
Busy rides grow queues. Add seats or speed them up, or the crowd gets grumpy and stops coming.
Rides sometimes break down. Tap Repair, or wait for the mechanics. Hire more mechanics to make it rarer.
Drag the park to look around, pinch to zoom and twist with two fingers to turn it. Tap a ride to manage it.
After $1 T in total earnings, sell your park for golden tickets: permanent bonuses for every park after.
