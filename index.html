How to play

Build rides on the lots and level them up. More fun brings more guests, and guests pay for every ride.
Busy rides grow queues. Add seats or speed them up, or the crowd gets grumpy and stops coming.
When the park is full, buy more land in the Park tab. Pop golden balloons for prizes.
After $100 M in total earnings, sell your park for golden tickets: permanent bonuses for every park after.
